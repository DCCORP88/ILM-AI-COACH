# Workflow: Loyalty and Referral Programs

**Status:** draft from Deanna's material, awaiting her review | **Version:** 0.1
**Sources:** source-notes/other_business_notes.md §2.3 (Lash'd loyalty program, referral options, prepaid annual package); rebooking-and-retention.md. This is Deanna's method unless marked [GENERAL GUIDANCE].
**Numbers that show it working:** loyalty members, returning clients, rebook rate, referrals, Google reviews, average spend.

---

## Principles (Deanna)
- **Loyal clients are paying your bills.** Nurture existing clients over constantly chasing new ones.
- A loyalty program rewards the behaviour you want: **coming back, rebooking, reviewing, referring and booking online.**
- It works alongside your policies, not instead of them (see salon-policies.md).

## Lash'd Eyelashes' program, as an example (*confirm it's current*)
| Element | Lash'd setting |
|---|---|
| Earn | Leave a review = 500 points · $1 spent = 2 points · online booking = 100 points · 10 visits = 1,000 bonus points |
| Tiers | Intro (0–500) · Loyalty (500) · Friends of Lash'd (2,000) · Prestige (5,000), each with its own perks |
| Redeem | 100 points = $1 in salon |
| Referral | Referrer gets 20% off their next appointment (spend over $100), and the friend gets 20% off |
| Booking | All bookings online |

## Program options to choose from (Deanna's menu)
- **Frequent visit:** e.g. a free touch-up after X full sets.
- **VIP spend tier:** perks once a client passes a spend level.
- **Birthday offer.**
- **Pre-booking reward:** a small reward for rebooking before leaving the chair.
- **Referral:** double-sided discount (e.g. 10% each), a free touch-up after 3 referrals, tiered rewards (1 = 5%, 3 = 10%, 5+ = free full set), or a seasonal "refer a friend" (e.g. a complimentary tint for the friend). Make it easy (cards, a booking link), track it and reward promptly.
- **Prepaid annual package:** e.g. 4 × Lash Lift + Tint and 4 × Brow Sculpt plus one free appointment, used within 12 months. *"No rushing to rebook. No price increases. No gaps where you feel undone."* Close it on a set date.

## Setting one up (steps)
1. Decide the behaviour you want most (rebooking, referrals, reviews or spend). Your numbers tell you which (see below).
2. Pick a simple structure. **[GENERAL GUIDANCE]** Start simple; you can add tiers later.
3. Cost it: work out what each reward costs you in product and time, and keep the total affordable. The coach uses the pricing worksheet for this, not guesses.
4. Set it up in your booking system (many, including Fresha, have loyalty features; check yours).
5. Tell every client at checkout, on the booking page and in your aftercare message.
6. Track members, returning clients, referrals and reviews monthly.

## How the coach uses this
**Triggers:** "how do I keep clients", "should I start a loyalty program", "referral ideas", "clients don't come back", "how do I get more reviews".

**Check first:** rebook rate, returning vs new clients, current reviews, average spend, and whether a program already exists and what it costs.

**Choose the focus from the numbers:**
- Low rebook rate → pre-booking reward plus the rebooking method.
- Few reviews → points for reviews.
- Few new clients but happy regulars → referral reward.
- Steady regulars, want cash flow → prepaid package.

**Then:** create a "Loyalty & referral program" project, set a baseline and target (e.g. referrals from 2 to 6 a month), add setup tasks, and log results monthly.

**Limits:** discounts and prepaid packages must follow consumer law (clear terms, expiry rules vary by state and country). Prepaid money may have tax and accounting treatment, so refer that to the accountant.
