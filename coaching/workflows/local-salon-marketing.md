## Principles (Deanna)
- **"You are a local business, so get local with locals."** Stop waiting for clients to find your social media page.
- **Fix retention before acquisition.** If clients aren't rebooking, new clients leak straight back out (see rebooking-and-retention.md).
- **Know your dream client.** Someone who loves your style, values your work, becomes loyal and will pay for quality. "You won't appeal to everyone and that's ok."
- **Message before marketing.** Use the 4 Clarity Questions (who, what, why now, why you) and "I help [WHO] achieve [OUTCOME] without [OBSTACLE]."
- **"Localise your threats."** Artists in other suburbs aren't your competition. Call local artists "colleagues in the same industry", not competitors.
- **Don't copy** another salon's marketing. Their success doesn't guarantee yours.

## The local marketing plan (4 weeks)

**1. Get the basics right** (before spending anything)
- A booking system that takes deposits and sends reminders ("stop relying on DMs").
- Branding that matches your dream client (e.g. "hot pink barbie" branding won't attract clients in an affluent area). Show every style you want to be booked for.
- **[GENERAL GUIDANCE]** A complete Google Business Profile: correct name, address, hours, services and prices, booking link, and recent photos.

**2. Reviews**
- Ask every happy client for a Google review, at the moment they're delighted with the result.
- Reward reviews through the loyalty program (Lash'd gives points for a review).
- Respond professionally to every review, including complaints: "how you handle a problem publicly defines your brand."

**3. Local partnerships** (pick two)
- Partner targets Deanna recommends: hair salons, wedding and event planners, photographers, gyms and fitness or pilates studios, medical spas, nail salons.
- Offer something useful to their clients (e.g. a welcome offer card) and track which partnerships bring bookings.

**4. Local events and community**
- Bridal expos, markets, charity events, fashion shows, networking. Bring cards, have an attendee-only offer, dress professionally, and follow up afterwards.
- **Authority Stack:** enter awards (credibility), speak at or attend events (exposure), educate others (authority), be in the room (network = growth).

**5. Local search**
- Use service terms plus your suburb or city on your website, Google profile and captions (e.g. "lash lift Ivanhoe").

**New-client specials (Deanna)**
- A costed, new-client-only special is a valid way to bring in new local clients. Deanna has run a discounted Korean lash lift for new clients via a Meta ad. See pricing-and-price-rises.md §6 for how to set one up, and judge it by how many come back at full price.

**6. Local paid ads (only once steps 1–4 work)**
Deanna's own salon setup (see digital-marketing.md for details):
- **Cold ad:** about a 5 km radius, video, sent to the Instagram profile so people get to know the salon, targeting your dream-client interests (e.g. pilates, fitness, beauty, wellness).
- **Retargeting ad:** people who engaged or viewed the profile or website, sent **straight to the booking page**.
- "Meta ads only work once the funnel is built."

## How the coach uses this
**Triggers:** "I need more clients", "nobody knows about my salon", "how do I market locally", "quiet period", "should I run ads".

**Check the numbers first** (from the Numbers page; ask only for what's missing):
1. Diary utilisation (visits ÷ available slots). If it's already high, marketing isn't the fix; look at pricing or rebooking.
2. Rebook rate. If it's low, fix retention first.
3. Enquiries per month, and how many booked (enquiry conversion). Few enquiries is an awareness problem; many enquiries but few bookings is a conversion problem (response time, booking link, price, message).
4. Where new clients currently come from, and Google review count.
5. Marketing budget (business-wide) and hours available.

**Then:** choose the **one** biggest gap and give a 4-week plan with weekly tasks. Set a baseline and target in a "Local salon marketing" project (e.g. enquiries from 12 to 20 a month) and log results weekly.

**Never:** promise a number of clients, invent local benchmarks, or recommend ads before the offer, profile and booking flow are ready.
