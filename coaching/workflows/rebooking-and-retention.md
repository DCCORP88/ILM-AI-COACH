## The principle

> Retention beats acquisition. Learning to keep your clients is easier than constantly finding new ones, and loyal clients are paying your bills.

Rebooking is a **habit you train into your clients**. When they rebook every time, they understand you're busy and can't be booked "whenever it suits".

## Deanna's rebooking method (Lash'd Eyelashes)

1. **Rebook at the end of every appointment, before the client leaves.** Don't leave it to a follow-up message.
   - From her courses: *"Do not ask clients if they want to re-book. Book them before they leave the chair. Offer the time, confirm it, and send a reminder."*
2. **Lock it in with a deposit** (50%, per salon-policies.md), so clients don't back out.
3. **Why it matters for the business:**
   - it fills the books ahead of time
   - it takes pressure off the team to find clients at the last minute
4. **If the client says "I'll book later":** let them, without pressure. Tell them honestly what the schedule looks like ("we're nearly full for the next few weeks"). Real availability creates urgency, **especially around Christmas** and other peak periods.
5. **Follow-up:** Fresha sends automatic reminders by text or email. Clients who haven't rebooked after **8 weeks** are followed up.
6. **Measure it:** Lash'd targets an **80% rebooking rate**, tracked on the Fresha dashboard.

### Rebooking intervals Lash'd teaches (*Deanna's figures, from her courses*)
| Service | Rebook in |
|---|---|
| Classic lash fills | 2–3 weeks (6+ weeks usually means a full removal) |
| Lash lift | 6–8 weeks |
| Lash tint | before the colour fully fades (lasts 3–6 weeks) |
| Brow tint | 4–6 weeks |
| Brow hybrid dye | 6–7 weeks |
| Brow lamination | 6–8 weeks, never more often |

### Rebooking line (from Deanna's lash lift course)
> "Let's lock in your refresh for six weeks so you're never without the lift."

**[PROPOSED]** Same pattern for other services, for Deanna to confirm:
> Lashes: "Your fill is due in two weeks — I've got [day/time] or [day/time]. Which works better?"
> Brows: "Your brows will be ready again in about five weeks. Shall I pop you in for [day]?"
> "I'll book later": "No problem! Just a heads-up, we're getting close to full for [month/Christmas], so let us know soon if you'd like a spot."

### Incentives Lash'd has used (*confirm which are current*)
- **Loyalty program:** points for reviews, spend, online bookings and visit milestones; four tiers; points redeemable in salon; a double-sided referral reward.
- **Prepaid annual package:** e.g. 4 × Lash Lift + Tint and 4 × Brow Sculpt plus one free appointment, used within 12 months. *"No rushing to rebook. No price increases. No gaps where you feel undone."*

## The biggest beginner mistake

> Not rebooking clients because you think they'll remember.

They won't, or they'll book whenever it suits them, and your diary becomes unpredictable. Get them into the habit from their very first appointment.

## How the coach uses this

**Typical triggers:** "my clients don't come back", "my diary is empty some weeks", "how do I get more repeat clients", "quiet before Christmas", "I'm always chasing bookings".

**Check first** (profile/project; ask only what's missing):
1. Does the owner rebook at the end of the appointment? Who does it, and what do they say?
2. Do they take a deposit on rebookings?
3. Current rebooking rate, if their booking system shows it. If not, how to find it. Never invent it.
4. Service mix, and therefore the intervals that apply.
5. What reminders and lapsed-client follow-up are set up?

**Then:**
- If they don't rebook in the chair, start there. It's the biggest lever.
- Give them rebooking lines in their own words for their services.
- Set up booking-system reminders and an 8-week lapsed-client follow-up.
- Where it fits, mention retail aftercare (Lash'd aftercare is one option; see COACH_INSTRUCTIONS §9): it protects the result, which brings clients back.
- **Next action:** rebook every client before they leave for the next 2 weeks. **Measure:** rebooking rate on the booking-system dashboard. Lash'd aims for 80%, but present that as Deanna's salon target, not an industry benchmark.

**Limits:** follow-up texts and emails must respect consent and unsubscribe rules (e.g. Spam Act in Australia). The coach works with segments and totals, not client contact lists.
