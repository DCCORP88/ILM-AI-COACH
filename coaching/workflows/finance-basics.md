## 2. The biggest money mistake (Deanna)

> Not putting money aside for tax and GST.

The money in your account isn't all yours. Part of it belongs to the tax office. Owners who spend it get a bill they can't pay.

## 3. Habits to coach

**From Deanna:**
1. Use proper tools from the start: accounting software (e.g. Xero) and booking-system reports, with a bookkeeper once you can afford one.
2. Set a **revenue target** and check revenue against it.
3. **Pay yourself a set amount.** Treat your own pay as a fixed cost (it's also built into your prices; see pricing-and-price-rises.md).
4. **Set aside tax and GST from every payment**, ideally in a separate account, so it's never spent by accident.

**[GENERAL GUIDANCE, Deanna to review]**
5. Know the difference between **revenue** (money in) and **profit** (what's left after all costs, including your wage). A busy salon can still lose money.
6. Watch a few simple numbers each **month**, in addition to revenue:
   - profit
   - product and consumables cost
   - staff cost
   - average spend per visit
   - rebooking rate (Fresha)
   - number of new vs returning clients
7. Keep a **buffer for slow months**, e.g. January or school holidays. The coach helps the owner decide how much with their own numbers, and doesn't invent a figure.
8. Ask your accountant **how much to set aside** for tax and GST, and how often to move it. It depends on the business structure and income.
9. Keep business and personal money in **separate accounts**.

## How the coach uses this

**Typical triggers:** "I'm busy but never have money", "I got a big tax bill", "how much should I pay myself", "what numbers should I track", "is my business making money".

**Check first** (profile/project; ask only what's missing):
1. What tools they use (accounting software, booking system reports, bookkeeper or accountant).
2. Whether they pay themselves a set amount.
3. Whether they set aside tax/GST (and whether they're registered for GST; the coach doesn't decide whether they need to be).
4. Whether they know their monthly revenue, costs and profit. If not, start there.

**Then:**
- Start with the biggest risk: if they're not setting money aside for tax and GST, that comes first.
- Help them set a revenue target and a simple monthly check-in using the dashboard (Business snapshot) and their own data. **Every metric shows its source and date. Missing values say "Add data".**
- Use app calculations for any numbers (revenue goal calculator, pricing worksheet), never arithmetic in the chat.
- **Next action:** e.g. open a separate tax account this week and ask the accountant what percentage to transfer. **Measure:** money set aside vs the expected tax/GST bill, and monthly profit.

**Limits:** no tax, GST, BAS, super, structure, deduction or investment advice. *"That's one for your accountant — here's what to ask them: …"* The coach can help the owner write their question list for the accountant.
